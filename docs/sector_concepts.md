# Sector Concepts

## Installed Capacity

Rated power of generation assets, modeled in MW. It is not energy generated.

## Generation

Effective production over time. ONS subsystem balance values are MWmed.

## Load

Power demanded by the system over a time interval. ONS load datasets used here refer to the SIN and are measured in MWmed.

## SIN

Sistema Interligado Nacional. It does not necessarily include every isolated system in Brazil.

## Submarkets / Subsystems

ONS data commonly uses Southeast/Center-West, South, Northeast and North subsystems. The project preserves official naming.

## Centralized Generation

Large-scale generation assets represented in SIGA and connected under centralized rules.

## MMGD

Micro and mini distributed generation connected at consumer units. It is not treated as centralized generation.

## MW, MWh and MWmed

MW is power. MWh is energy. MWmed is average power over a period. The project does not sum these units together.

## PLD

Preco de Liquidacao das Diferencas, expressed in R$/MWh.
