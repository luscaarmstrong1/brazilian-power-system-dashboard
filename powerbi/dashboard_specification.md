# Dashboard Specification

## 1. Brazilian Power System - Executive Overview

Objective: show system size, renewable share and main geographic concentration. Cards: total capacity, renewable share, solar, wind, hydro, MMGD, SIN average load, annual added capacity. Visuals: source mix, historical capacity, capacity by region, Brazil map.

## 2. Generation Matrix

Objective: compare installed capacity with effective ONS generation. Visuals: capacity by source, generation by source, capacity vs generation share, generation trend and source ranking.

## 3. Power System Load

Objective: explain SIN demand behavior. Cards: average load, peak load, latest data date. Visuals: load by subsystem, daily trend, monthly curve and hour/day heatmap where hourly data is available.

## 4. Renewable Energy

Objective: track hydro, wind, solar and biomass. Visuals: renewable capacity share, source growth, geography and generation trend.

## 5. Distributed Generation

Objective: analyze MMGD expansion. Cards: MMGD MW, system count, average system size. Visuals: UF ranking, source split, consumer class, yearly additions and map.

## 6. Geographic Analysis

Objective: locate generation assets and concentration. Visuals: plant map, choropleth by UF, top states, top municipalities and top plants. Filters: source, UF, region, situation and power band.

## 7. Expansion

Objective: identify sources and regions leading capacity growth. Visuals: added MW by year/source, YoY growth, CAGR, UF expansion ranking.

## 8. Energy Market

Objective: show PLD when official CCEE data is available. Visuals: monthly average PLD, min/max bands and submarket comparison. If unavailable, page states the official-access limitation and focuses on SIN market context.

## 9. State Detail

Objective: drill through by UF. Shows total capacity, state matrix, MMGD, solar, wind, hydro, thermal, undertaking count, top plants and expansion history.

## 10. Source Detail

Objective: drill through by grouped source. Shows capacity, UFs, evolution, plants, centralized/distributed separation and generation comparison where applicable.

## Interactions

Global filters: year, source, UF, region and situation. Tooltips must show unit, source, scope and latest data date. Drill-through pages use UF and source.
